## Scope

Whole web UI (Control, Stats, Automations, Settings, Logs). Mode: Operate. Desktop browser tab first, phone must work. Build path: code-first (app screen).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Desk is drawn, not reported. Height is a live dimension line on a side elevation of the Desk; Presets are level markers you click. Refuses the default: coloured badges, blue button grid. The large Height figure stays: the user accepted the built Control screen ("Jest ok, ale biurko nie wygląda jak biurko Idasen" — "It is ok, but the desk does not look like an Idasen desk"); their only objection was the drawing.

OWN-WORLD: Drafting vellum off-white ground, graphite ink, 1 px hairline rules, square corners, no cards or shadows. Technical-lettering sans with tabular figures. One red-pencil colour, reserved only for "Desk moving" and Stop (kept from terminal wayfinding). Dark mode: inverted vellum, white ink on graphite, not blueprint navy.

STORY: The owner glances and knows what the Desk does now and what Automation comes next; acts only to move, stop or pause.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: wordmark, tabs, Connection and Paused status. Left: elevation drawing, dimension line with Height, dashed Stance-threshold datum, level markers per Preset (click to move), hold-to-move Up/Down beside it, Stop always visible. Right: "Next" table of upcoming Automations in fixed columns, today's sit/stand totals below. Phone: drawing on top, table below. Stats: one plot owns the field (kept from Factory sleeve). Whole layout from 1 px hairlines (kept from design-annual plate).

FORM: Architectural section drawing with dimension lines and level markers; position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:9a1e5d977f834845.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
